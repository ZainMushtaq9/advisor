# Phase 10 — Sector indicator profiles

Profiles are framework notes (which indicators matter for a sector and
why), stored in `sector_metric_profiles` with `metric_key =
"macro:<indicator>"`. They are NOT company exposure claims: only the
exposure register (issuer-evidenced) makes company claims.
